### Description

World War I did not start the day Archduke Franz Ferdinand was shot. For weeks, there was no war.

The timeline: the assassination on 28 June 1914, Germany's promise of support to Austria-Hungary in early July (the "blank cheque"), the 48-hour ultimatum to Serbia on 23 July, Austria-Hungary's declaration of war on 28 July, then Russia, Germany, France and, on 4 August, Britain.

### Description

In 1914, the German, Austro-Hungarian, Russian and Ottoman empires ruled much of Europe and the Middle East. Four years later, all four were gone.

New countries appeared, including Poland, Czechoslovakia and Yugoslavia, borders were redrawn, and resentments over the settlement helped bring another world war within a generation. Roughly nine to ten million soldiers had died.
